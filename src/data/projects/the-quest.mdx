## Overview

**HMS Core** is a comprehensive Hospital Management System designed to digitize and streamline hospital operations. It manages **patients**, **doctors**, **appointments**, **billing**, and **administration workflows** in a single unified platform, improving efficiency, accuracy, and overall patient care.

## Mission Statement

> **MISSION** - Build a complete, scalable Hospital Management System that supports real-world hospital workflows and is ready for production use.

## How it works

Hospital staff interact with the system based on their roles. Admins manage departments, users, and system settings. Doctors handle appointments, patient records, and prescriptions. Reception and billing teams manage registrations, OPD/IPD billing, and reports — all through a centralized dashboard.

## Key Features

### **End-to-End Hospital Management**

- **Patient Management**
- **Doctor & Staff Management**
- **Appointment Scheduling**
- **OPD / IPD Billing**
- **Reports & Analytics**

### **Role-Based Access System**

- **Admin Panel**: Full system control
- **Doctor Dashboard**: Patients, prescriptions, schedules
- **Staff Access**: Registration, billing, records
- **Security**: Permission-based data access
